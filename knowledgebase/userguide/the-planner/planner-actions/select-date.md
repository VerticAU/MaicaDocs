## How does the Fortnight period work?

When the Planner **Period** is set to **Fortnight**, the Planner shows 14 days starting from the beginning of a week. The first day of the week follows the **Start Day of Week** in [Planner Management](https://app.gitbook.com/s/9selzjEx6KX7RYEawAVr/settings/planner-management).

The Fortnight period is not linked to your organisation's pay fortnight. Which 14 days you see depends on how you navigate:

| Action            | Fortnight shown                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Today**         | The current week and the following week                                        |
| **Select Date**   | The week containing the date you pick, and the following week                  |
| **Next/Previous** | Moves exactly 14 days forwards or backwards from the fortnight you are viewing |

For example, on Wednesday 23 September 2026, **Today** shows 21 September to 4 October. Pressing **Previous** once shows 7 September to 20 September.

### Viewing your pay fortnight

To display a specific pay fortnight, use **Select Date** and pick a date in the **first week** of that fortnight. For example, to view a pay fortnight of 14 to 27 September 2026, pick any date from 14 to 20 September.

Once your pay fortnight is showing, **Next/Previous** keep you aligned to your pay fortnights, because they move exactly 14 days at a time.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Picking a date in the **second** week of a pay fortnight shows the wrong 14 days. For example, picking 22 September 2026 shows 21 September to 4 October. Pressing **Today** can also move you off your pay fortnight, depending on the current week.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
The fortnightly overtime check uses the **Overtime Fortnight Anchor Date**, not the fortnight shown on the Planner. Resource hours shown on the Planner cover the dates on screen, so they can differ from the hours counted towards a Resource's Fortnightly Hours Limit. To learn more, see [Rostering Management](https://app.gitbook.com/s/9selzjEx6KX7RYEawAVr/settings/rostering-management).
{% endhint %}
