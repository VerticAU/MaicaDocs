# Legacy Experience

{% hint style="info" %}
Maica offers two experiences for creating and managing Appointments, **Standard** and **Legacy**. The experience you see depends on your organisation's settings. If your screen does not look like the one described below, contact your administrator.
{% endhint %}

In the Legacy experience, an Appointment is created through a series of stages. Each stage covers one part of the Appointment, and the stages are completed in turn before the Appointment is confirmed.

## Appointment stages

After the new Appointment is created, a few stages must be completed before the Appointment can be confirmed.

{% hint style="info" %}
The available stages are configurable and determined by the selected [Appointment Services](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/appointment-service.md) and the Maica Settings. For further information, see the Administration Guide.
{% endhint %}

Below is a list of these stages:

1. [Basic Details](general/basic-details.md)
2. [Location](general/location.md)
3. [Schedule](general/schedule.md)
4. [Additional Details](general/additional-details.md)
5. [Custom Fields](general/custom-fields.md)
6. [Summary](general/summary.md)

To dive deeper into each stage, visit the specific page for detailed information on that stage.
