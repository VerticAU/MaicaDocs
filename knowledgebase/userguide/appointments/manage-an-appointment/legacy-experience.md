# Legacy Experience

{% hint style="info" %}
Maica offers two experiences for creating and managing Appointments, **Standard** and **Legacy**. The experience you see depends on your organisation's settings. If your screen does not look like the one described below, contact your administrator.
{% endhint %}

## Opening an Appointment for editing

To begin managing an Appointment, click on the Appointment in your Planner. To change the details, click the `edit` button in the bottom right corner of the Manage Appointment screen. Until you click this button, all fields are `read-only`.

## Which Appointment sections can I manage?

Once editing, you are able to manage the following sections:

### [Basic Details](../create-an-appointment/general/basic-details.md)

In this section you can manage the [Participant(s)](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/participant.md), [Resource(s)](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/resource.md) and [Asset(s)](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/asset.md) allocated to your selected Appointment. You can do this by typing the name of a Participant, Resource or Asset (or multiple), or by clicking on the `Filter` icon, which allows for [Smart Selection](../create-an-appointment/general/smart-selection-filter.md) of any of the above.

You can also adjust the Date & Time information as well as the Type of [Appointment](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/appointment.md). This is aimed at showing you quickly who is attending and who is delivering services, on what date and at what time.

### [Appointment Services](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/appointment-service.md)

This section allows you to manage your existing Appointment Service(s) as well as add new Appointment Service(s) if needed.

The following fields are manageable:

| Number | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | Click this button to add an additional Appointment Service(s).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 2      | Change the quantity of an [Appointment Service](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/appointment-service.md) to control how long each [Participant](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/participant.md) may spend at an [Appointment](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/appointment.md). The quantity of an Appointment Service is further explained [here](../create-an-appointment/general/summary.md#id-1.-adjusted-quantity). |
| 3      | Click this button to refresh any changes to your Appointment Service, including an adjustment of quantity. This will update any relevant information, including the Service cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 4      | This number outlines the number of Appointment Service(s) that are connected to your Appointment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 5      | Click this button to mark this Appointment Service as cancelled. This will exclude this Service from your Appointment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the selected Participant(s) do not have an Agreement for the added Appointment Service, an error will occur. This error is further detailed [here](../create-an-appointment/general/basic-details.md#id-2.-participant-s-have-no-agreement-for-selected-appointment-service).
{% endhint %}

### [Location](../create-an-appointment/general/location.md)

This section enables the editing of the Location at which the Appointment is being held, including a lookup to an existing Location, a Participant Mailing Address, a manually-entered Address, or a Digital Location for this Appointment only.

{% hint style="success" %}
When managing Location, there is a Recalculate Travel button located in the top right hand corner of the modal. This allows you to refresh travel times and distances directly within the modal, without needing to update the Origin/Destination values or reload the modal.
{% endhint %}

### [Additional Details](../create-an-appointment/general/additional-details.md)

The focus of this section is to edit the [Shift(s)](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/shift.md) and [Checklist(s)](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/checklist.md) applicable to this Appointment. The Shift lookup is filtered by active Shift(s) at the same [Location](../../getting-started/maica-key-concepts/location.md) that the Appointment is being held.

### [Custom Fields](../create-an-appointment/general/custom-fields.md)

The Custom Fields tab captures any information from Custom Fields that have been added to your organisation's Appointment profile beyond what **Maica** offers. This section allows for the management of already added fields, **not** the addition of new fields.

## Saving your Appointment

Once you have made the changes, either click the `Tick` icon to confirm your changes and update your Appointment, or the `Undo` icon on the bottom left of the screen to cancel your changes.
