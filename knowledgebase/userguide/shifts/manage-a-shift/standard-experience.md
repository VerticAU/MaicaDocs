# Standard Experience

{% hint style="info" %}
Maica offers two experiences for creating and managing Shifts, **Standard** and **Legacy**. The experience you see depends on your organisation's settings. If your screen does not look like the one described below, contact your administrator.
{% endhint %}

In the Standard experience, an existing Shift opens in a single screen with two steps. In the first step you review and change the details, editing each one in place. In the second, the **Summary** shows the Shift Services that will be delivered before you save.

## Opening a Shift

To open a Shift, click on it in your [Planner](../../the-planner/planner-overview.md).

The Shift opens ready to edit. Each detail can be changed directly, subject to your permissions, with no separate edit step. The **Actions** menu sits beside the **Shift Details** heading at the top of the details.

Where a Shift has been Completed or Cancelled, a banner above the details states this, and only the actions that still apply are offered.

The footer shows where you are in the process, as **Step 1 of 2** or **Step 2 of 2**.

## The Actions menu

The **Actions** menu holds everything you can do with the Shift beyond editing its details. It is available at any time while the Shift is open, including while you are making changes. Each action is only listed when it applies to the Shift and to you.

| Action                    | When it is listed                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Check-In**              | When the Shift can be checked into                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Check-Out**             | When the Shift can be checked out of, including once it is Completed                                                                                                                                       |
| **Quick Complete**        | When the Shift can be quick completed                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Cancel**                | When the Shift can be cancelled                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Shift Notes**           | When you have permission to manage notes, including once the Shift is Completed or Cancelled                                                                                                               |
| **Checklist**             | When check-in and check-out are available and the Shift has Checklists to complete during delivery                                                                                                         |
| **Attach Files**          | When Shift files are enabled for your organisation                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Manage Shift Break**    | When Shift breaks are enabled for your organisation                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Manage Shift Expenses** | When Shift expenses are enabled for your organisation, including once the Shift is Completed                                                                                                               |
| **Shift Incidents**       | When you have permission to manage incidents                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Open Shift**            | When you have permission to open Shift records. The record opens in a new tab                                                                                                                              |
| **Directions from...**    | When Google Maps is configured for your organisation. Directions are offered from the Home Address, the Primary Location and the Previous Shift, where each has an address, and from your Current Location |

Once a Shift is Completed or Cancelled, **Check-In**, **Quick Complete**, **Cancel**, **Checklist**, **Attach Files**, **Manage Shift Break** and **Shift Incidents** are no longer listed. **Check-Out** and **Manage Shift Expenses** remain available on a Completed Shift, but not on a Cancelled one.

Each action opens its own dialog. To learn more about each one, see [Shift Actions](../shift-actions/).

## Step 1: reviewing and changing the details

Each detail of the Shift is a row. Click a row to open it, make your change, and move on to the next. When you have finished, click **Next** to move to the Summary.

The rows are the same as when a Shift is created, other than **Shift Type** and **Status**, which are set when the Shift is created and are not shown here. To learn what each row holds, see [Standard Experience](../create-a-shift/standard-experience.md) under Create a Shift.

### Schedule

Where the Shift belongs to a recurring series, the **Schedule** row shows a one-line summary of the recurrence, and opening it shows the schedule's details.

| Indicator               | Meaning                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open Schedule**       | Shows whether this is the Master Shift of its schedule or one Shift in the series. Click it to open the Schedule record |
| **Unapproved Schedule** | The schedule has not been approved, so it will not generate ongoing Shifts until it is                                  |
| **Approve Schedule**    | Shown in place of Unapproved Schedule when you have permission to approve schedules. Click it to approve the schedule   |

To make a one-off Shift repeat, click **Add Schedule** in the row. To learn how to set up the schedule, see [Standard Experience](../create-a-shift/standard-experience.md) under Create a Shift.

## Step 2: the Summary

The Summary shows what will be delivered before you save. At the top are the **Date and Time**, the **Location** and, where the Shift repeats, the **Schedule**.

Beneath them, the Shift Services are listed with their status. Cost, Quantity and Ratio are not shown for a Shift, and the Funding Source selector is not available.

## Saving your changes

Click **Save** to save your changes. While Maica is checking the Resources and Services, **Next** and **Save** are greyed out. Anything that stops the Shift being saved is listed above the details.

Where the Shift belongs to a recurring series, Maica asks on save whether your changes apply to this Shift only or to all future Shifts in the series. To learn how each option affects the schedule, see [Manage a Shift](./).
